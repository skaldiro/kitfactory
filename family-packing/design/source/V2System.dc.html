Design system · v1.0 · Nov 21, 2026

kitmunk

The single source for every screen. If a screen and this board disagree, this board wins and the screen gets fixed.

Rule 1

Orange means Scout.

A button is orange only when it hands work to Scout or applies what Scout proposed. One per screen at most. Never on progress, tags or links.

Rule 2

Dashed means a guess.

Anything Scout inferred gets a dashed orange line or a dashed card, so people know what to check.

Rule 3

Everyone is a circle.

Adults and kids look the same: a crew color and an initial. Age is the caption, not a different shape.
