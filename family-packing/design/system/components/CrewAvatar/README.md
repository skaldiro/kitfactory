# CrewAvatar

Everyone is a circle: crew fill, a darker ring, an initial.

Adults and kids look the same; age is the caption (Adult, 6 yrs, Under 1), worked out from birth month and year. Sizes 28, 40, 48, 64. With `progress` (0 to 1) the ring becomes that person's packing progress. Shared items use `shared` and the initials Us.
